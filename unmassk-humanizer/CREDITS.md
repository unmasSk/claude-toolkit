# Credits

`unmassk-humanizer` is a fusion of prior open work. It would not exist without:

- **humanizer** by blader (MIT) — https://github.com/blader/humanizer. Based on
  Wikipedia's "Signs of AI writing". Source of the content-pattern catalog, the
  false-positives / human-signals guidance, and the adversarial self-audit loop.

- **humanize-writing** by lguz (MIT) — https://github.com/lguz/humanize-writing-skill.
  Source of the three-pass process, the named voices, and the pre-return checklist.

- **anti-slop** by kjmagnan1s (MIT) — https://github.com/kjmagnan1s/anti-slop.
  Source of the three modes, the protect-list seam, the living corpus, transition
  iteration, and context profiles. Itself a consolidation of:
  - **avoid-ai-writing** by Conor Bronsdon (MIT) — tiered vocabulary, context
    profiles, severity tiers.
  - **stop-slop** by Hardik Pandya, hvpandya.com (MIT) — false-agency rule,
    binary-contrast variant table, 5-dimension scoring rubric.
